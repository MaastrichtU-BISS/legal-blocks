Clause 14 - Termination and notice

14.1 The tenant may terminate this agreement by giving the landlord written notice of not less than one calendar month. Notice shall take effect on the first day of the month following the month in which it is received.

14.2 The landlord may terminate this agreement only on the grounds set out in Article 7:274 of the Civil Code, and shall give the tenant written notice of not less than three calendar months. That period is extended by one month for each full year the tenant has occupied the property, to a maximum of six months.

14.3 Notice under this clause shall be sent by registered post to the address stated in Schedule 1, or delivered by hand against a signed receipt. Notice sent by electronic mail is valid only where the receiving party has confirmed receipt in writing.

14.4 Where the tenant dies during the term, any person who occupied the property as their principal residence together with the tenant may continue the tenancy, provided they notify the landlord in writing within six months of the date of death and can demonstrate a durable joint household.

14.5 If the tenant vacates the property without giving notice as required by clause 14.1, the tenant remains liable for rent until the date on which notice would have taken effect had it been given on the day of departure.
